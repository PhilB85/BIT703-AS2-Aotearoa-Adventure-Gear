About Aotearoa Adventure Gear

Helping Kiwis explore the great outdoors with confidence, comfort and quality gear.

Our Story

Aotearoa Adventure Gear is inspired by New Zealand's incredible natural landscapes, from mountain ranges and native forests to beautiful coastlines.

We believe outdoor adventures should be enjoyable and accessible, whether you're heading away for a weekend camping trip or preparing for a challenging hike.

Our aim is to help outdoor enthusiasts find practical equipment for their next adventure.

What We Offer

Hiking Equipment

Explore backpacks, hiking boots and practical accessories for outdoor trails and adventures.

Camping Essentials

Discover tents, sleeping bags and equipment designed for comfortable camping experiences.

Outdoor Accessories

Find useful accessories such as water bottles and headlamps to complete your outdoor kit.

Adventure Starts with Preparation

Whether you're exploring a local walking track or planning an overnight camping adventure, having suitable equipment can make all the difference.

Aotearoa Adventure Gear brings together a selection of outdoor products to help customers prepare for their next journey.
